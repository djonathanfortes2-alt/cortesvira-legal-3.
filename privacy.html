# cortesvira-legal-3. Privacy Policy - Cortes Vira

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how the operator of Cortes Vira handles information when using the video workflow and its integrations with Postiz and TikTok.

Information processed

Depending on the features used, the workflow may process TikTok account information made available through authorization, such as basic profile information, and videos, captions, and publishing details that the operator chooses to submit.

How information is used

Information is used to connect the authorized account, prepare and manage video posts, and support publishing requested by the operator.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by TikTok, Postiz, and other service providers necessary to operate the workflow, subject to their own privacy policies and terms. This policy does not claim that no data is shared.

Storage and security

Information may be handled on the operator's computer and by connected services. Reasonable steps should be taken to protect account credentials and access tokens. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Information should be kept only as long as needed for the stated purposes. The operator can disconnect integrations using the available account settings and may request applicable data deletion from the relevant service providers.

Your choices

You can choose which content to submit and can revoke TikTok authorization through the options TikTok provides.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through the GitHub profile.
